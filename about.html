About Me

A short introduction to who I am and what I hope to create.

Hello, I'm Alex!

They say that 13 is an unlucky number, some even fear this because of the ideas associated with it. But my mama said that this number brought her luck — it brought me to her. Hello there ! I am Alexandria Renee Gonzales Iligan, a 21 year old Multimedia Arts student in FEU Tech. Being the eldest daughter in the family, I always do my best to become a role model and a good sister to my younger siblings. I am a woman who is passionate with her craft and who makes sure she walks with kindness, knowledge, and grace. I believe that learning is endless and that there's nothing wrong in trying new things.

I aspire to become a Creative Director someday. As I step foot in the creative field, I want to explore opportunities that will help me develop her skills, gain additional knowledge, and grow as a creative professional. My goal is to create meaningful storyteling, develop strong design, and produce impactful, visually engaging work that inspire others.

Interests: Sketching & Painting, Reading, Music, Photography

Skills: Concept Development, Graphic Design, Visual Composition, Spatial & Design Awareness, Creative Thinking

Goal: To grow within the creative field through meaningful storytelling, developing strong design and creative skills, and producing impactful, visually engaging work that inspires others.
