My Creative Archive

Creativity begins with a simple idea—but it takes curiosity, experimentation, and persistence to bring it to life. As a Multimedia Arts student, I explore different ways of communicating through graphic design, illustration, photography, animation, and visual storytelling. Each project gives me a chance to experiment, make mistakes, learn, and discover what kind of creative I want to become.

GRAPHIC DESIGN

IDILY: Reimagined

A reimagined version of the film "I'm Drunk, I Love You" poster. I explored its typography, colors, composition, and mood while adding my own creative style.

ILLUSTRATION

Cami The Go Pixie

A whimsical character illustration of Cami, a cheerful fairy character. I focused on her soft colors, detailed wings, outfit, and gentle expression to create a cute and playful design.

ANIMATION

Pitcher and Cup

A short and playful animation featuring a pitcher and a cup in a kitchen setting. I focused on their expressions, character design, and simple movements to make the scene feel lively and entertaining.

3D MODELING

Hirang's Weapon

A 3D modeling project focused on creating Hirang’s weapon. I worked on its shape, details, and overall design to make it match the character’s style.

PHOTOGRAPHY

Reminiscence

Photography allows me to capture moments, emotions, and details that can easily be overlooked. Through each frame, I explore composition, light, and perspective to turn ordinary moments into visual stories.

BRANDING & EDITORIAL DESIGN

AVAIAN

A magazine design project focused on creating an organized and visually appealing layout. I explored typography, images, colors, and page composition to create a cohesive editorial style.
